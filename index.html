Live Cinematic platformer 2D + WebGL

Inferno

Dante’s Inferno, canto by canto, restaged as a descent through a subway system — in the exact grammar of Another World (1991). Hell is the transit network under the city, Virgil is a station attendant, and every circle is a deeper, stranger line. Seventeen screens, then the story keeps going in real 3D: Purgatorio, a Three.js chapter with dynamic shadows and a rising dawn.

  • Tap to shoot · hold to shield · long-hold to charge
  • Narrated, with subtitles
  • Score descends a root note per circle
  • Touch controls on phones
